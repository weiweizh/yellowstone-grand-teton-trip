## Design Context

### Users
Travelers using the itinerary mostly on mobile while moving through Yellowstone and Grand Teton, with occasional desktop planning. They need quick orientation, memorable stop details, directions, and a low-friction way to track what they have seen.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, grounded, quietly adventurous. The experience should feel like a well-made field journal rather than a generic booking dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial field journal: cinematic landscape imagery, warm paper tones, deep forest ink, strong typographic hierarchy, and tactile route details. Mobile-first, light mode, minimal motion.

### Design Principles
- Make the next stop obvious at a glance.
- Use landscape imagery as an emotional anchor, not decoration.
- Keep touch targets generous and controls reachable.
- Let itinerary content breathe with clear progressive disclosure.
- Use motion sparingly and respect reduced-motion preferences.
